**Project:** [OMICRON](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/955269) (EU H2020, GA 955269)

**My role**

- Designed a cooperative multi-UAV inspection framework in which each UAV runs its own onboard Behavior Tree, from mission upload to landing.
- Validated at the Aznalcóllar bridge (Spain) and deployed on two DJI Matrice 210 V2 at the in-service Settefonti motorway viaduct (Italy).

**Related paper:** [ICUAS 2025](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICUAS65942.2025.11007792)
