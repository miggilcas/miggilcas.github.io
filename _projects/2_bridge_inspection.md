Each UAV carries its own onboard Behavior Tree and executes its share of the inspection mission, from mission upload to landing.

- Validated at the Aznalcóllar bridge (Spain).
- Deployed on two DJI Matrice 210 V2 at the in-service Settefonti motorway viaduct (Italy), within the EU H2020 project OMICRON.

**Paper:** [ICUAS 2025](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICUAS65942.2025.11007792)
