**Project:** [AERIAL-CORE](https://aerial-core.eu) (EU H2020) · **When:** 2022–2023

**My role**

- Hardware/software integration of commercial platforms (2× DJI Matrice 210 + DeltaQuad Pro VTOL) with custom onboard hardware and open-source tools into one heterogeneous team.
- Flew in the project's final demonstration (ATLAS Flight Test Center, Jaén, Oct. 2023): two multirotors inspected three lines simultaneously, covering 15 km in under 15 minutes.

**Related paper:** [ICUAS 2024](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICUAS60882.2024.10556864)
