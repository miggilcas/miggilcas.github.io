Integrated commercial multirotors (2× DJI Matrice 210) and a DeltaQuad Pro VTOL with custom onboard hardware and open-source tools into one heterogeneous team for long-range power-line inspection.

- At the EU H2020 AERIAL-CORE final demonstration (ATLAS Flight Test Center, Oct. 2023), two multirotors inspected three lines simultaneously, covering 15 km in under 15 minutes.
- The same fleet was deployed in Doñana National Park (project RESISTO) to confirm or dismiss alarms raised by the power grid's sensor network.

**Paper:** [ICUAS 2024](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICUAS60882.2024.10556864)
